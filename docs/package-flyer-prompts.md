# Package flyer creative brief

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool using the three customer-supplied flyers as references. Full-resolution PNG originals and optimised JPEG website copies are in `assets/packages/`. Prices are rendered as live, accessible website text rather than embedded in the artwork.

## Shared prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Redesign the supplied Wakanow / Headies 2026 Toronto travel flyer into a refined, editorial luxury travel campaign. Portrait 4:5 composition. One single finished flyer, not a mockup. Brand palette dark ink navy #061a33, Wakanow electric blue #008bc7, orange #f78220 and restrained champagne-gold. Elegant asymmetrical Swiss layout, enormous crisp typographic hierarchy, generous whitespace, coherent baseline and grid, polished magazine cover quality. Preserve the Wakanow logo identity and the distinctive golden Headies trophy from the reference. Toronto waterfront and CN Tower photo in the middle occupying ~45%, golden trophy on right partly crossing that photo. Top small Wakanow logo + 'THE HEADIES 2026'. Big 'TORONTO' headline. Clearly legible package name underneath. Bottom organized white/ivory panel, small gold line icons and concise text. NO hotel photographs or fabricated specific properties, NO exact price (live current prices appear in the website UI beside this flyer), NO dates, NO star rating symbols on trophy or implied specific properties. Do not reproduce crowded original collage. Footer call to action 'Explore the package' and exact URL 'headies.wakanow.com'. Small footer 'See current prices and booking details online.' Only include specified text and brand logos.
